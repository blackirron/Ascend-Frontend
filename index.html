About Ascend

A first credit record for students, built safely.

Ascend helps college students in India start a credit history without a debt trap. A refundable deposit unlocks a small revolving line from a regulated partner lender. Every cost is shown before the student spends, and on-time repayment is reported to credit bureaus.

Starting limit1.5× depositIllustrative. Set with the lender.

Joining, annual, processing₹0No hidden charges.
Our late fee income₹0The ₹50 late fee goes to the lender.
The problem

No history, no credit. No credit, no history. Banks reject first-time borrowers because there is nothing to score, so young adults fall back on a parent's card or on instant-loan apps that charge heavily and recover harshly.

Our deck cites that 8 in 10 young applicants are rejected by traditional banks. Harassment, hidden charges and data misuse by some lending apps have also made students and their parents distrust anything that promises instant credit.

Who we build for

Ishaan, 20, second-year engineering student in Hyderabad. He gets about ₹6,000 a month from his parents and a little more, irregularly, from social-media work for small shops. He wants independence, discounts on what he already buys, and a credit score before his first job. He fears getting trapped in debt.

He sits between our other two personas and leans toward neither, so he is the cleanest test of whether a structured, controlled path to credit works. We prove it with him first.

The product in seven steps
1. Sign upMobile number and one-time code. Nothing else is asked until it is needed.
2. DepositAge and college are verified with DigiLocker. The student puts in a refundable deposit, for example ₹5,000, held by the partner bank in their name.
3. Initial limitThe partner lender sets a small limit of 1.5 times the deposit. The student can use all of it, in one spend or many.
4. See total costInterest, fees and penalties in plain words, with the worst case shown before the student spends.
5. Spend itDiscounts on food, clothes and the categories the student chooses. Money goes from the lender to the student, never through Ascend.
6. Repay on timeEach spend is due in 30 days. On-time repayment is reported to credit bureaus.
7. View score and new limitA credit index and a new limit are offered. The limit only grows if the student accepts.
How we decide: two signals

Standard checks and salary slips cannot judge most young applicants, so we use two factors instead of a credit score.

IntentThe refundable deposit. It shows the student means to repay and gives the lender a baseline of trust.
CapabilityConsented monthly inflow totals from bank statements, checked against what the student tells us. We use the lower number. College ID confirms the student profile.
GateMinimum inflow of ₹4,000 a month to open a limit. This is an assumption to confirm with the lender.
What it costs
Interest-free window30 days from each spend. Repay in full and pay ₹0 extra.
If not repaid in full18% a year after the due date, about ₹22.50 a month on ₹1,500. Assumed.
Late fee₹50, flat, once per spend. It goes to the lender, not to Ascend. No compounding.
Penalty interestNone beyond the normal rate.
Other fees₹0 joining, annual, processing and over-limit. No cash withdrawal.
Worst case on ₹1,500Repaid 30 days late: ₹1,572.50.

Rates and fees are illustrative. The lending partner sets the real ones, and taxes apply as required.

How Ascend earns

We earn when users repay on time, not when they struggle.

Month 0 onwardFee on performing accounts, paid by the lending partner.
Month 1 onwardShare of card economics, paid by the lending partner.
Month 1 onwardMerchant-funded offers, paid by merchants.
Month 12 onwardGraduation referral to a larger partner bank.
How we will know it works
North Star6 on-time in a rowEarly indicator: 3 in a row. Targets: 50% reach 3 by month 4, 35% reach 6 by month 8.
RiskAt least 92%Share of users who repay within the grace period. It should not go below 92%.
BusinessRevenue per active userMeasured over six months.
Deposit drop-offIf more than 40% leave at the deposit step, we lower the minimum deposit.
Good users leavingIf more than 15% of good users leave within 6 months because of the cap, the lender reviews the cap.
OverspendIf a user's monthly spend jumps 30% and they miss a payment, new spending pauses until they catch up.

The partner lender console in this prototype shows these measures on a simulated cohort of about 200 users.

Scope and roadmap
Now: pilotOne city cluster, Hyderabad. One partner lender. One starter line with a refundable deposit. Short tips in the app.
NextA second partner. Bigger limits for proven users. Short custom videos on the topics students actually need.
